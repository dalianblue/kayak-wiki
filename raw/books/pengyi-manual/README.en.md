# PengYi FeatherCraft Kayak Series

![PengYiLogo](img/%E6%8F%92%E5%9B%BE01.png)

> Start Your Adventure Journey 🚣

The PengYi FeatherCraft series is a skin-on-frame folding kayak, inspired by Alaskan kayak touring design. Lightweight, portable, and easy to assemble and store.

---

## Features

![羽船39](img/%E6%8F%92%E5%9B%BE02.png)

- **Lightweight & Portable** - Net weight only 13.2-15.5kg, compact package size
- **Comfortable Suspended Seat** - 12mm thick closed-cell foam backrest and cushion
- **Premium Materials** - 6063-T6 aluminum frame + TPU 4-layer deck + Hypalon hull
- **Quick Assembly** - No tools required, easy assembly following illustrated steps
- **Easy Storage** - Disassembles into a backpack, store in cool dry place

## Quick Assembly Guide

![羽船组件](img/%E6%8F%92%E5%9B%BE03.png)

Assembly takes just 20 steps. For detailed instructions, see the [Full Manual](羽船系列说明书.md):

1. **Install Bow/Stern Frame** - Secure small formers to frames, insert footrest
2. **Unfold Skin** - Open Velcro straps, slide frame into skin
3. **Install Keel Extension Rods** - Connect bow and stern, extend and lock
4. **Install Cockpit Rim & Seat Sling** - Slide into designated positions
5. **Install Middle Sleeve Connector** - Align tubes and connect
6. **Install Large Formers** - Place and snap into position
7. **Install Deck Rails** - Insert through former holes and lock
8. **Install Seat & Inflate** - Secure seat webbing, inflate air chambers

## Maintenance & Care

### Frame Maintenance
- Disassemble after 2-3 months of use, rinse inside and out with fresh water
- Spray lubricant on tube assembly positions
- Complete disassembly and maintenance every two months

### Skin Maintenance
- Remove water with sponge after paddling
- Avoid dragging on sharp rocks
- Small damages can be repaired with patch kit

### Storage Requirements
- Keep clean and dry
- Store assembled or disassembled
- Extension rods must be completely separated

---

## Optional Accessories Installation

### Rudder
Insert rudder pin into keyhole, rotate 180°, connect control lines to footrest.

### Sea Sock
Waterproof safety accessory prevents cockpit flooding. Shiny side faces frame, logo on right.

### Storage Hatch
Connect hatch ring through deck fabric sleeve, secure with buckle.
